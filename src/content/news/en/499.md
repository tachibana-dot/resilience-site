				<p>On Friday, June 9, 2023, the Hokkaido Bureau of Economy, Trade and Industry of the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry and LEVANES Corporation held the "Regional Problem Solving Seminar" in Sapporo, attended by about 100 people, including local government officials and small and medium-sized business people (online delivery was also available).</p>
<p>The purpose of this program is to explore new ways to solve regional problems by matching local governments struggling with various "issues" with startups and other companies with advanced technologies.</p>
<p>Charenergy Corporation and WOTA Corporation participated in this seminar.</p>
<p>Chalenagy Corporation (Tokyo, Japan)<br />
Disaster-resistant, quiet, bird-friendly micro wind turbine that can be utilized in cold regions</p>
<p>WOTA Corporation (Tokyo, Japan)<br />
Small-scale decentralized water circulation system" for domestic wastewater reclamation</p>
