<p data-path-to-node="3"><b data-path-to-node="3" data-index-in-node="0">Report] "Science Castle World 2025" Evolves into a World Convention, Offering a Multifaceted View of the Shape of Resilience</b></p>
<p data-path-to-node="4">On December 13 and 14, 2025, Science Castle, the gateway to success for junior and senior high school researchers, reached a major turning point. The format that had previously been held at various chapters in Japan was merged into "Science Castle World 2025," bringing together young researchers from around the world.</p>
<p>Convention website:<a href="https://castle.lne.st/schedule/science-castle-world-2025/">https://castle.lne.st/schedule/science-castle-world-2025/</a></p>
<p data-path-to-node="5">More than 200 abstracts from all over Japan and Southeast Asian countries such as Malaysia, Singapore, the Philippines, Indonesia, and Vietnam gathered at Science Tokyo, the venue for the conference.</p>
<p data-path-to-node="6">It should be noted that the competition became "WORLD," making visible the "different approaches of each country and region" to common issues.</p>
<p data-path-to-node="6">For example, even in the area of "environmental conservation," knowledge of ecosystem maintenance in urban areas in Japan and mangrove regeneration technology in coastal areas of Southeast Asia were discussed in the same forum. By exchanging each other's on-the-ground wisdom, participants were able to learn about issues in other countries that they had not known before, and new ideas were born.</p>
<p data-path-to-node="7">Here we have selected 16 abstracts related to "urban development, environment, and disaster prevention" that support our daily lives. These are studies based on the curiosity of junior and senior high school students, who have been walking around the sites on their own and steadily accumulating data.</p>
