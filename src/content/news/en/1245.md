				<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">On Thursday, May 21, 2026,<a href="https://www.helicalfusion.com">Helical Fusion, Inc.</a>The “Helix Project Partnering Event in Kansai,” organized by Helical Fusion (hereinafter “Helical Fusion”) to invite partners to join the “Helix Project” aimed at launching a fusion energy industry in Japan, was held. Ribance Co., Ltd. (hereinafter “Ribance”) planned and managed this event.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The event was attended by approximately 40 guests, including representatives from companies considering participation in the fusion energy industry. An official partner of the Helix Project,<a href="https://youtu.be/xmjzZmie6ZY?si=StiHUH6T2KMXsOIi">Concept Video</a>The event, which kicked off with a presentation by Mr. Taguchi, CEO of Helical Fusion, on the overall vision of the “Helix Project*”—an initiative designed to promote cross-regional and cross-sectoral collaboration—also featured an overview of the latest trends in the fusion energy industry, as well as a presentation and discussion by Nichias Corporation, which participated as the first “Official Partner*.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Following the discussion, participants split into three groups focused on the themes of the “Helix Project,” “Manufacturing,” and “Official Partnership Participation,” where they engaged in Q&amp;A sessions and discussions aimed at gaining a deeper understanding and envisioning collaboration based on their respective areas of interest. This provided each participant with an opportunity to specifically consider potential avenues for collaboration—such as operational and capital partnerships—between their own companies and the fusion energy industry.</span></p>

<p><strong>*What Is the Official Partner Program?*</strong></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This program designates partner companies that proactively promote the “Helix Project”—aimed at achieving practical power generation using fusion energy—in collaboration with Helical Fusion as “Official Partners.” Official Partners enter into technical and business alliances, as well as capital partnerships exceeding a certain threshold, and share a commitment to tackling the challenge of realizing the fusion energy industry with creativity and perseverance. Specifically, they will jointly advance the manufacturing and construction of the final demonstration facility, Helix HARUKA, and the first power-generating unit, Helix KANATA, as well as related initiatives.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">First Group of Participating Companies: Nichias Corporation, Hase-Tora Spinning Co., Ltd., and Seno Kisen Co., Ltd.</span></p>
