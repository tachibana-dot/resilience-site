				<p>We extend our heartfelt sympathies to all those affected by the earthquake that struck Kumamoto Prefecture on July 28, 2026.</p>
<p>Yamada Shokai Holdings Co., Ltd., a partner of the Tokai Chapter of the Lifeness Resilience Project, and Terra Lab Co., Ltd., a subsidiary of L-VENTURES, will collaborate with the private drone disaster response organization “JUIDA-D³ (Drone Disaster Dispatch)” to begin collecting wide-area disaster damage data using aircraft and drones.</p>
<p>The survey covers Kumamoto City and its surrounding areas. Its purpose is to quickly assess the extent of damage during the initial response to a disaster and to share information with government agencies, disaster prevention organizations, infrastructure operators, and others.</p>
<p><strong>Announcement by Terra Lab</strong><br />
<a href="https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000077.000034117.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Response to the Kumamoto Earthquake (Second Report)</a></p>
<h2>Survey of Disaster Damage Using Two Aircraft and a Drone</h2>
<p>The aerial survey will be conducted using a two-aircraft system consisting of an advance party aircraft and a wide-area survey aircraft.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Advance Party Aircraft:</strong>We will be among the first to enter the disaster-stricken areas to assess the damage through aerial photography and gather initial information.</li>
<li><strong>Wide-Area Survey Aircraft:</strong>We will conduct wide-area surveys using high-precision cameras to generate digital maps and gain a comprehensive understanding of the extent of the damage.</li>
</ul>
<p>In addition, Terra Lab Fukushima will dispatch a drone team to conduct detailed surveys of areas that are difficult to inspect by aircraft, as well as key facilities and roads.</p>
<p>The two aircraft are scheduled to depart from Nagoya Airport on July 29 and fly to Kitakyushu Airport, where they will begin full-scale aerial surveys and drone surveys in Kumamoto City and the surrounding areas starting the following day, July 30.</p>
<h2>Integrating Aviation Data and Lifeline Information</h2>
<p>Data collected by aircraft and drones is integrated and analyzed in real time using a GIS (Geographic Information System).</p>
<p>Furthermore, by integrating information on essential services and other data collected by Yamada Shokai Holdings, we will establish a “Common Operational Picture (COP),” which is essential for disaster response.</p>
<p>The COP we have developed will be provided to government agencies, disaster prevention organizations, infrastructure operators, and others, with the aim of supporting rapid decision-making during the initial response to a disaster—including assessing the full extent of damage, conducting search and rescue operations, clearing roads, and restoring essential services.</p>
<h2>Collaboration in the Tokai region, built up over time</h2>
<p>In December 2024, Ribane Co., Ltd., together with Terra Lab and Yamada Shokai Holdings, launched the “Ribane Resilience Project Tokai Chapter,” which aims to create flexible and resilient social infrastructure in anticipation of natural disasters such as the Nankai Trough earthquake.</p>
<p><strong>Announcement Regarding the Launch of the Tokai Chapter</strong><br />
<a href="https://www.yamada-hd.co.jp/news/688/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">In collaboration with Lifeness and the Terra Lab Disaster Response Operations Center, we have launched the Tokai Chapter of the “Lifeness Resilience Project.”</a></p>
<p>Terra Lab is conducting research and development on a “Disaster Response Information System” that uses aircraft to quickly and accurately collect extensive 3D data and share that information with various organizations, including government agencies, local authorities, and infrastructure operators.</p>
<p>For over 100 years, Yamada Shokai Holdings has been involved in essential infrastructure services such as gas, water, and electricity. In the event of disasters such as earthquakes and floods, the company has gone directly to the affected areas to assist with the restoration of these essential services.</p>
<p>In December 2024, the two companies entered into a research and capital partnership to jointly advance the research, development, and field deployment of a wide-area disaster response operation system.</p>
<p>The response to the recent Kumamoto earthquake is a collaborative effort that combines Terra Lab’s technologies for aerial observation, drones, AI analysis, and GIS-based information integration with Yamada Shokai Holdings’ expertise in daily living infrastructure and on-site response capabilities.</p>
<h2>Establishing a Coordination System That Can Respond During Disasters</h2>
<p>After a disaster strikes, there are limits to how quickly we can respond if we have to gather the necessary technology and organizational resources from scratch.</p>
<p>The Lifeness Resilience Project believes it is important for companies, startups, research institutions, government agencies, and local communities to collaborate on a regular basis, combining their respective technologies, information, and on-site response capabilities to build a system that can be effectively activated in the event of an emergency.</p>
<p>Going forward, we will continue to share information on disaster response efforts by various companies—including the activities undertaken this time—and, based on the situation in the affected areas and the needs on the ground, we will promote collaboration to leverage the technologies of the companies and startups participating in the project for recovery and reconstruction efforts.</p>
