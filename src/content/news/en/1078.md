<p>Yamada Shokai HD and LEVANES spoke at the "Opinion Exchange Meeting for Establishment of Disaster Countermeasure Information Support Network in Case of Nankai Trough Earthquake" held at Nagoya Airport Terminal Building on March 11, 2025.</p>
<p>The "Exchange of Opinions Meeting for the Establishment of a Disaster Countermeasures Information Support Network for a Possible Nankai Trough Earthquake" is,<a href="https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000052.000034117.html">Terra Labs Disaster Response Operation Center</a>The event took place at Terra Lab is a start-up company that conducts research and development of a wide-area disaster response operation system using the long-range unmanned aerial vehicle Terra Dolphin, and will establish its operation center on December 13, 2024. At the same time, the company is preparing a scheme to utilize drones in times of disaster, "Aichi Prefecture's Version Drone Disaster Response Model," while studying business models not only in emergency situations but also in peacetime. At the same time, we are preparing a scheme to utilize drones in times of disaster.</p>
<p>At this opinion exchange meeting, Mr. Toyohisa Yamada, President of Yamada Shokai HD, gave a case study presentation titled "Infrastructure Construction Businesses' Efforts to Prepare for the Nankai Trough Earthquake," in which he explained their efforts to prepare for disasters from ordinary times.</p>
<p>which was launched last December by Yamada Shokai HD, Liverness Corporation, and Terra Labs Inc.<a href="https://resilience.lne.st/en/743/">Resilience Project Tokai Chapter</a>Nishimura of Liverness, Inc. introduced about</p>
<p>Mr. Hiroyuki Suzuki, Senior Managing Director of Yamada Shokai HD, also participated in the discussion that followed, exchanging opinions with the other speakers on the roles of government, companies, and communities in times of disaster, and how information networks should be established that leverage the strengths of each.</p>
<p>The LIVERNESS Resilience Project will continue to communicate with local businesses, ventures, and municipalities to maintain and strengthen the resilience of the region, and will strive to create new projects.</p>
