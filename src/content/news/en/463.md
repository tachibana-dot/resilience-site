				<h2><strong>April 19 Press Conference] Launched the "Resilience Project" to create a tolerant social system that is compatible with both peacetime and emergency situations.</strong></h2>
<p>(Head Office: Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo; Group CEO: Yukihiro Maru) is pleased to announce the launch of the "Riverness Resilience Project," which aims to create a flexible and tolerant social infrastructure that can function in both emergencies and peacetime through cross-industrial collaboration among partners from different industries, ventures, government, research institutions, and other fields. The "LIVERNESS Resilience Project" will be launched.</p>
<p>Under the sponsorship of RIVANES, East Japan Railway Company, Aqua Clara Corporation, and Canon Marketing Japan Inc. will participate as partners in the first phase, and seven ventures with technologies such as self-sustaining distributed infrastructure, food security, rebuilding, and satellite data, and one Together with an academic team, we plan to develop business solutions related to disaster management.</p>
<p><strong>⬛︎ Current planned development themes</strong><br />
Autonomous decentralized infrastructure systems (water, energy)<br />
WOTA Corporation / Charenergy Corporation / Environmental Microbiology Laboratory Co.<br />
Large-Scale Fire Prevention System<br />
UTokyo Team / Ridge-i Corporation, etc.</p>
<p>In the future, we will solicit themes related to resilience issues from Ishikawa, Kumamoto, and other local governments, and form project teams from different industries and fields by combining technologies possessed by venture companies and universities, and assets of medium-sized and large companies.</p>
<p>Therefore, a press conference will be held on Friday, April 19, from 4:00 p.m.</p>
