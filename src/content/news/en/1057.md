				<p><img fetchpriority="high" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-medium wp-image-1058" src="/images/imported/news/en-1057-0.png" alt="" width="800" height="533" />The Hyperdisciplinarity Conference Tokyo 2025, hosted by RIVERNESS, will feature the session "Shrines, Temples and Resilience" (session partner: Yamada Shokai Holding Co., Ltd.).</p>
<div>In December 2024, Yamada Shokai Holding, a company that develops lifestyle infrastructure businesses based on gas, water, and electric work, conducted an inspection of a temple with a small drone in cooperation with Liberaware, a company that develops the world's smallest drone, and Ninna-ji Temple, a World Heritage site in Kyoto Prefecture. The project was conducted in cooperation with Ninna-ji Temple, one of the World Heritage sites in Kyoto. Can cutting-edge technology be used for buildings that are supposed to be protected, as opposed to modern buildings? Resilient urban development will be discussed.</div>
