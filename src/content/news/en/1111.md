<h3><strong>Program Overview</strong></h3>
<p>JP-MY Resilience Xchange Program (RXP) is a co-creation platform for startups, researchers, and companies in Japan and Malaysia to collaborate and face disaster and environmental challenges.<br />
We aim to create new solutions to flooding and water resource problems, which are increasing due to climate change, by bringing together the technologies and knowledge of both countries.</p>
<h3><strong>background</strong></h3>
<p>Rapid urbanization and climate change in Malaysia have resulted in frequent flooding and other disasters.<br />
After the floods, issues such as water pollution and drainage system failure have been highlighted and require immediate action.</p>
<h3><strong>Japan's Strengths</strong></h3>
<p>Japan has cultivated some of the world's most advanced resilience technologies through its experience in the face of diverse disasters such as earthquakes, tsunamis, and torrential rains.<br />
Earthquake-resistant buildings, tsunami embankments, rescue and risk assessment technologies using drones and robots, and early warning systems based on weather data will also make a significant contribution to solving Malaysia's problems.</p>
<h3><strong>Program Objectives</strong></h3>
<p>Promotion of mutual understanding: Understanding each other's culture, business practices, and technical methods.<br />
Integration of Japanese technology: Accelerate utilization through demonstration, co-creation, and localization in Malaysia.<br />
Establishment of issue-specific working groups: Formation of international working groups for water resources and climate change issues.<br />
Support for overseas expansion of Japanese companies: Support strategic overseas expansion through ecosystem building and public-private partnerships.<br />
Strengthening long-term cooperative relations: Strengthening Japan-U.S. cooperation in environmental and water-related fields to contribute to the sustainable development of the region.</p>
<h3><strong>phase expansion</strong></h3>
<p><strong>Phase 1 (establishment of working groups)</strong><br />
Academia, startups, and companies bring their expertise to co-create issue-specific strategies.</p>
<p><strong>Phase 2 (project pitch)</strong><br />
Propose jointly developed solutions to stakeholders and proceed to demonstration.</p>
<p><strong>Phase 3 (project implementation)</strong><br />
Long-term development through MoU signing, field trials, and co-creation with local communities.</p>
<h3><strong>Themes to be resolved</strong></h3>
<p>Water Pollution Countermeasures<br />
Flood control and adaptation<br />
Management of leaks and unaccounted for water</p>
<h3><strong>Expected Value</strong></h3>
<p>Expand networking among academics, startups, and businesses<br />
Early access to advanced technologies<br />
Market understanding and brand communication<br />
Contribution to solving social issues<br />
Practical educational opportunities for students</p>
