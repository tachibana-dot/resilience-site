<p><b><br />
Background of Implementation<br />
</b>RIVANES Corporation, Focus Systems Corporation, and Environmental Microbiology Laboratory, Inc. have launched the RUMEN Project (RUMEN: Resilient system Under disaster by green Methane ENergy) to develop a small-scale methane fermentation system with disaster response capabilities.<br />
This project aims at social implementation of a resource-recycling power generation system, centered on the "Eco Standalone," a stand-alone small methane fermentation system proposed by the Research Institute for Microbiology of the Environment, in order to realize an energy supply that can promptly supply gas and electricity in times of disaster and be close to the hearts of the people.<br />
<a href="https://lne.st/2024/01/15/rumen/">https://lne.st/2024/01/15/rumen/</a></p>
<p>Currently, with the cooperation of PLANT Corporation, we have installed the first demonstration unit we developed at the PLANT-3 Kawakita store and are conducting a demonstration test of methane fermentation using vegetable scraps discarded from the grocery store to produce city gas and electricity.</p>
<p>Through the implementation of this experimental class, we aim to widely publicize our efforts to the local community in the vicinity of the PLANT-3 Kawakita store, so that they will be effectively utilized in the event of an emergency.</p>
