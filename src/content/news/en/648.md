<p>Liberaware Inc., one of the participating venture companies in this project, received a letter of appreciation from the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry for its assistance in surveying collapsed houses and restoring lifelines using IBIS2, an indoor inspection drone, in the Noto Peninsula earthquake.</p>
<p>We would like to express our deepest respect for the company's quick response and disaster relief activities using advanced technology, and we sincerely hope for the earliest possible recovery of the disaster-stricken areas.</p>
