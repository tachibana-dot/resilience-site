				<p>Last year, RIVANES launched the RIVANES Resilience Project in response to the Noto Peninsula earthquake on New Year's Day, 2024, in order to mobilize the power of Deep Tech to create a resilient city that can withstand natural disasters.</p>
<p>One year has passed since we started our activities this month, but even now, the need for resilient community development continues to grow due to abnormal weather conditions and a series of natural disasters centered in Noto.</p>
<p>In order to deliver resilience-related solutions to local governments and related ministries, we have organized solutions by category, posted them on our website, and launched a searchable site.<br />
We are aiming for a release at the end of February next month.</p>
<p>After its release, the site will be promoted to all local governments as the only site in Japan that gathers resilience-related solutions.</p>
<p>If you have a solution you would like to see listed on this site, please send us your information using the form below.</p>
<p><strong><a href="https://id.lne.st/project/resilience_info_registration/regist/basic_info/1">Resilience Solutions Portal Information Form</a></strong><br />
*You need to log in to your LIVERNESS ID.</p>
<p>The items we would like you to provide are as follows</p>
<ul>
<li>Service/Solution Name</li>
<li>Service Overview</li>
<li>Strengths and Appeal Points</li>
<li>Case Studies (Including Demonstration Results)</li>
<li>How to use in peacetime</li>
<li>Materials (optional)</li>
<li>Basic company information (company name, location, year established, company logo)</li>
<li>1 image (photo/illustration)</li>
</ul>
<p>We would appreciate it if you could contact us with any comments or questions you may have.</p>
