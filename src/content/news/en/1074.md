<p>(Nonoichi City, Ishikawa Prefecture, President: Yasunori Baba, hereinafter "Environmental Microbiology Research Institute"), Focus Systems Corporation (Shinagawa-ku, Tokyo, President: Kei Mori, hereinafter "Focus Systems"), and LIVANES Co. (Head Office: Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo; President and COO: Shuichiro Takahashi) have launched the RUMEN Project (RUMEN: Resilient system Under disaster by green Methane ENergy) in January 2024 to develop a compact methane fermentation system with disaster response capabilities. We recently held an experimental class for elementary school children and their parents to observe the first demonstration unit for social implementation, which was installed at a local supermarket.</p>
