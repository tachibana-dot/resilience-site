				<p>We extend our heartfelt sympathies to all those affected by the Reiwa 8 Kumamoto Earthquake.</p>
<p>WOTA Co., Ltd., which is also collaborating with L-VENTURES on the Ribance Resilience Project, has begun providing bathing and hand-washing support in areas affected by water shortages, following an official request from Kumamoto Prefecture, with the aim of maintaining sanitary conditions. (From the company’s Facebook page)</p>
<p>As of 6:00 a.m. on July 29, 2026—two days after the disaster struck—it was reported that approximately 84,000 households were without water in 12 municipalities across three prefectures, primarily in Kumamoto Prefecture.</p>
<h2>Uki City Begins Offering Bathing Services Using Water-Recirculating Showers</h2>
<p>On July 30, 2026, WOTA completed the installation of the “WOTA BOX”—a water-recycling shower that can be used even during water outages—in Uki City, Kumamoto Prefecture, and began providing bathing facilities to evacuees.</p>
<p>"WOTA BOX" is a system that purifies and recirculates used water on the spot, enabling showers even in environments where tap water is unavailable.</p>
<p>We plan to continue installing them in stages at evacuation centers, medical facilities, and welfare facilities in areas affected by water outages.</p>

<h2>Approximately 400 units of domestic water supply equipment and materials have been consolidated in Kumamoto Prefecture</h2>
<p>WOTA is also making preparations to provide hand-washing facilities using its water-recycling hand-washing station, “WOSH.”</p>
<p>Currently, under the framework of the Japan Water Association for Disaster (JWAD), approximately 400 units of domestic water supply equipment have been consolidated in Kumamoto Prefecture, and we are proceeding with their deployment to evacuation centers, medical facilities, and welfare facilities in areas experiencing water outages.</p>
<p><strong>WOSH</strong><br />
<a href="https://wota.co.jp/wosh/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"> https://wota.co.jp/wosh/ </a></p>
<h2>Response based on a wide-area mutual aid agreement concluded prior to the disaster</h2>
<p>This assistance is being provided under the “Agreement on Regional Mutual Aid for Domestic Water Supplies and Equipment in the Event of a Disaster,” which was signed by WOTA and Kumamoto Prefecture on July 8, 2026.</p>
<p>This agreement aims to promote the establishment of a “Wide-Area Inter-Municipal Mutual Aid Platform” that uses prefectures as hubs to consolidate and deploy domestic water supply equipment and materials held by local governments nationwide to disaster-stricken areas.</p>
<p>Establishing methods for inter-municipal cooperation and systems for the deployment of equipment and supplies before a disaster strikes will enable rapid assistance after a disaster occurs.</p>

<p>Through the Lifeness Resilience Project, we will establish technologies for the sustainable use of water in everyday life and build regional operational frameworks to create a system capable of delivering water essential for daily life even during disasters.</p>
