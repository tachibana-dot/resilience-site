<p data-start="116" data-end="259"><strong data-start="116" data-end="137">Singapore, May 23, 2026</strong> — Hosted at the Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT), Ribane Singapore, in collaboration with its partner companies,<strong data-start="219" data-end="250">TECH PLANTER Singapore 2026</strong> We held the event.</p>
<p data-start="261" data-end="390">This program is a pitch platform that brings together researchers, startups, innovators, and industry leaders tackling challenges in the deep tech field. On the day of the event, diverse teams from the region—including Singapore—presented their initiatives to solve social issues through science and technology.</p>
<p data-start="392" data-end="449">Following passionate pitches and a rigorous judging process, the following teams—recognized for their outstanding innovation and future growth potential—were awarded the various prizes.</p>

<h2 data-section-id="1rl3rcu" data-start="456" data-end="485">Grand Winner Award | Etavolt</h2>
<p data-start="487" data-end="572"><strong data-start="487" data-end="497">Technology Overview</strong><br data-start="497" data-end="500">Etavolt is committed to developing sustainable energy solutions aimed at addressing key challenges in the fields of clean energy and electrification.</p>
<p data-start="574" data-end="642"><strong data-start="574" data-end="580">Consolation Prize</strong><br data-start="580" data-end="583">A cash prize of 3,000 Singapore dollars, and <strong data-start="603" data-end="636">TECH PLANTER Asia Finals 2026</strong> Qualification for</p>
<p data-start="644" data-end="790"><strong data-start="644" data-end="652">Reason for the Award</strong><br data-start="652" data-end="655">The field in which Etavolt operates is highly specialized, yet it addresses issues that are crucial to realizing a sustainable future. The judges highly praised the company’s technology for its relevance to the times, its social necessity, and its future scalability. The expectation of a long-term impact was also a decisive factor in awarding it the Grand Prize.</p>
