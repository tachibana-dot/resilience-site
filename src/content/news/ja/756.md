<p>リバネスレジリエンスプロジェクトでは、<a href="https://hic.lne.st/en/archive/my2024/">超異分野学会マレーシア大会2024</a>にて、特別パネルセッション「Tech Innovation in Disaster Resilience」を実施します。</p>
<p><strong>title:Tech Innovation in Disaster Resilience</strong></p>
<p>Tech Innovations in Disaster Resilience is a multifaceted topic that encompasses various aspects of utilizing technology to enhance disaster management and recovery efforts. The strategies for promoting resilience to mitigate the impact of disasters, such as infectious disease, droughts, floods, or wildfires. Exploring the role of technology and innovation in enhancing sustainability in agriculture, healthcare and infrastructure sector.</p>
