										<div class="solution__art">
						<h6 class="solution__artSubTit">summary</h6>
						<p>We are developing a compact tower-type heat-and-power cogeneration ZEH system with a rotational tracking system that can be installed even in conditions where roof-mounted solar equipment cannot be installed. Even with a small footprint, the system can use high-performance infrared absorption and a water-cooling mechanism to provide hot water, and the light-concentrating power generation cells enable high-efficiency power generation. In addition, this technology will contribute to improving disaster preparedness in many ways, such as desalination and ultraviolet sterilization.</p>
					</div>
					<!--技術面における強み-->
										<div class="solution__art">
						<h6 class="solution__artSubTit">Technical Strengths</h6>
						<p>The main features of this technology are a cylindrical solar concentrator with a group of rotating mirrors of about 1 square meter and the versatility and multifunctionality of switching the heat source power light source.<br><br>
The ability to automatically track in the direction of the sun from sunrise to sunset has increased the operation rate by more than 1.5 times compared to conventional fixed systems, and it also has an application switching function, making it possible to prepare for a variety of disasters.</p>
					</div>

						<h6 class="solution__artSubTit">How to use in peacetime</h6>
						<p>Steady energy savings because the light can be used as natural lighting indoors under normal circumstances.<br><br>
In addition, because it can deliver natural light containing appropriate amounts of UV and IR indoors, it is highly valuable as an office light source for health care applications such as the production of vitamin D in the body and prevention of autonomic nervous system disorders, as well as in school gymnasiums because it can be expected to promote calcium absorption.</p>
