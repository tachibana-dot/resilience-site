										<div class="solution__art">
						<h6 class="solution__artSubTit">summary</h6>
						<p>Space Waterworks Bureau is a service for municipal waterworks bureaus to support decision-making in the selection of survey points in the event of a leakage accident or when conducting surveys to prevent leakage. Based on the diagnosis results, the service divides the area under its jurisdiction into sections of approximately 100m square and visualizes the risk of leakage on a 5-point scale. The system can narrow down areas with high leakage potential, making it easy to find priority areas for investigation. Notes and photographs can be attached to the map for efficient investigation.</p>
					</div>
					<!--技術面における強み-->
										<div class="solution__art">
						<h6 class="solution__artSubTit">Technical Strengths</h6>
						<p>Pipeline diagnosis technology analyzes "leakage risk" by making full use of multimodal AI based on space big data, environmental big data (open data), and various information such as water pipeline information and leakage history. This technology, which also conforms to the "Basic Plan for Water Circulation," makes full use of the advantages of space big data, such as wide-area coverage, continuity, and disaster tolerance.</p>
					</div>

										<div class="solution__art">
						<h6 class="solution__artSubTit">How to use in peacetime</h6>
						<p>The system supports the formulation of pipeline renewal plans by evaluating the current status of water pipelines based on industry-standard asset management and risk management methods. Multiple "priority scenarios for renewal" are created by combining the "soundness" based on the results of the pipeline diagnosis and the "importance" that comprehensively considers the impact on all facilities and lifestyles, from general residences to critical water supply facilities. The system is also effective in sharing and centrally managing information among water department staff, and can be accessed from outside of the agency because the data is provided via the cloud.</p>
