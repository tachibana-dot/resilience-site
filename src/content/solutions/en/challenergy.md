										<div class="solution__art">
						<h6 class="solution__artSubTit">summary</h6>
						<p>Developing a next-generation wind turbine that can generate electricity even in strong winds and turbulent conditions, without propellers, providing high durability, low noise, and less concern for bird strikes. They wind turbine have generated electricity during typhoons and Challenergy Inc. are currently conducting demonstrations with Tokyo Metropolitan Government on wind turbines that are easy to install and relocate. Challenergy Inc. aims to install these wind turbines in disaster-prone areas domestically and internationally as renewable energy sources, to provide a secure and safe energy supply even during disasters.</p>
					</div>
					<!--技術面における強み-->
										<div class="solution__art">
						<h6 class="solution__artSubTit">Technical Strengths</h6>
						<p>Chalenergy's small wind turbine Type D uses a vertical-axis Savonius-type wind turbine to ensure stable power generation even when the wind direction changes.<br><br>
Special structure to catch wind in high winds, providing durability against damage even at high wind speeds.<br><br>
Furthermore, the low-noise design allows installation in busy urban areas.<br><br>
Hybrid operation with solar power generation and high customizability support a wide variety of applications.</p>
					</div>

										<div class="solution__art">
						<h6 class="solution__artSubTit">How to use in peacetime</h6>
						<p>Chalenergy's small wind turbines can be used for nighttime LED lighting, clocks, surveillance cameras, smartphone charging, and other applications. They can also be used to supply power to satellite communication equipment, contributing to the maintenance of a stable communication environment. The wind turbine can be easily installed in locations where it is difficult to build a power transmission infrastructure, and features the ability to utilize clean energy while keeping running costs low.</p>
