										<div class="solution__art">
						<h6 class="solution__artSubTit">summary</h6>
						<p>Developed the "e6s" sustainable flush toilet system, which recycles toilet flushing water and at the same time reduces the volume of waste for hygienic and safe recovery. e6s is working to prevent people from ever having to experience "toilet unavailability during a disaster" again.</p>
					</div>
					<!--技術面における強み-->
										<div class="solution__art">
						<h6 class="solution__artSubTit">Technical Strengths</h6>
						<p>e6s" is a flush toilet system that recycles toilet flushing water and collects waste in a hygienic and volume-reducing manner.<br><br>
The strength of "e6s" is its maintenance design, which focuses on "sustainability" and allows users to maintain and manage the system themselves. This is the latest "phase-free" technology that enables a completely different approach from bio-, botton-, and septic tanks, which are said to be difficult to maintain and manage.</p>
					</div>

						<h6 class="solution__artSubTit">How to use in peacetime</h6>
						<p>If e6s is installed and set up in advance, people can continue to use their daily public toilets or toilets at home even when infrastructure is disrupted by a disaster. Flush toilets can also be installed outdoors or in other locations where there is no infrastructure.</p>
