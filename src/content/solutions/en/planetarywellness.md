										<!--概要-->
										<div class="solution__art">
						<h6 class="solution__artSubTit">summary</h6>
						<p>Early detection of people with a tendency toward loneliness and guide them to early support by predicting their mental and physical conditions using AI for estimating mental states such as loneliness and environmental factor analysis technology. Provide a place to stay and show the way to hope through volunteer activities such as maintenance of Pecan Forest and renovation of vacant houses. Aim to solve social isolation and loneliness and create sustainable local communities through the use of data.<br><br>
</p>
					</div>
					<!--技術面における強み-->
										<div class="solution__art">
						<h6 class="solution__artSubTit">Technical Strengths</h6>
						<p>Natural language processing AI estimates and visualizes psychological states such as individual loneliness non-invasively and in real-time from everyday text and voice data. Environmental factor analysis technology predicts and visualizes mental and physical states up to a week into the future using weather, air pollutants, space weather, etc. This is an innovative technology for early awareness and early intervention in the unaffected stage.<br><br>
</p>
					</div>
